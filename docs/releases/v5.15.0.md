You control what the sidebar shows. Hover any item and an x appears to tuck it away; a new section in settings brings hidden items back, and the choice survives reloads for every item, not just some. The Activity switch in settings now actually hides Activity, and the bulk bar tucks its rarely-used Plan all and Implement all buttons into a tidy three-dot menu.

Helper transcripts finally have real names. The transcript list used to title every conversation with the same instruction-sheet leftovers; titles now come from the actual task being worked on, old junk titles were cleaned up across the whole history, a helper's transcript page always shows that helper's own conversation instead of someone else's, and an AI refusal can never become a stored summary again.

The helper board stopped crying wolf. Three working helpers were wrongly marked finished in two days because a few quiet minutes inside one long step looked like death. The board now checks real evidence before flagging anyone: a helper with no proof of death needs twenty quiet minutes in its own activity log, a helper whose process is confirmed gone is still flagged fast, and a wrongly flagged helper that reports in gets revived instead of forced to re-register under a new name. Activity byte counts on the Agents page read each helper's own log now, so they are real numbers.

Creating a draft can no longer freeze the app. Draft creation quietly asked the AI service to write the acceptance checklist and would wait up to ten minutes if that service hung. Callers that opt out of AI help now skip the call entirely, and the real call gives up politely after thirty seconds and falls back to the built-in template.

The Activity page's Events and What I learned tabs show real data again, and six screens that silently failed to load after a routing slip (a doubled /api in their addresses) are fixed with a guard so the mistake cannot come back.

Smaller touches: long task titles on the themes page fold to two lines with an expand control, minesweeper got beveled tiles and a framed board to match the classic reskin, and the retired Text yourOS feature no longer clutters settings.
