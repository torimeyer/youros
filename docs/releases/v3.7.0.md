# myOS v3.7.0

## What's new

**Flag it, reply from Inbox.** Flag a Slack message for follow-up. It lands in your Inbox. Open a reply right there, let Gemini Enterprise draft it, edit if you want, and send. The loop is one page, not three.

**Unified Inbox.** Everything that needs your attention in one place: flagged Slack messages and tasks that came in from outside. Dismiss what you've handled, convert anything to a task, or reply without leaving.

**Gemini Enterprise replies.** Your organization's Gemini writes Slack replies in your voice. One click to draft, yours to edit, one more to send.

## How to update

```
cd ~/myos
./update.sh
```
