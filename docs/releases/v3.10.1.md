# yourOS v3.10.1

**Two fixes from the 3.10 release.** Your ADHD mode setting now follows you across devices, and the Settings nav keeps things where they belong.

## What's better

### ADHD mode sticks across devices
Turning ADHD mode on in one browser used to reset the next time you opened yourOS on a different device or browser. The setting now lives on the server, so it follows you everywhere.

### Settings sections stay in the right place
Data Management and Shared Links were showing up on every Settings tab, not just Privacy & Data. They now appear only where they belong, so the Settings sidebar is easier to scan.

## How to update

```
cd ~/myos
./update.sh
```
