## How to use this

1. Click each **→ Open** link to see the file in Drive.
2. To move a file: either drag it into the right folder in the Drive left sidebar, or right-click on it in Drive and pick "Move to", then choose the destination folder.
3. To delete a file: open it, then in Drive right-click it and pick "Move to trash".
4. Work through sections in any order. Check off each line as you finish it.
5. Files in the "for us" folder are left alone on purpose. Skip anything you see there.
